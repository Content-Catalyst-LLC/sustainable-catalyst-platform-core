# Platform Core v2.11.0 — Humanitarian Access & Essential Services Fabric

v2.11.0 adds a provenance-preserving humanitarian-condition fabric on top of the v2.10.0 facility registry. Health, education, food, water, electricity, fuel, displacement, communications, shelter and humanitarian-access conditions remain separate observations with explicit semantic roles.

Key safeguards: structural baselines are not current operational conditions; report metadata is not automatically promoted to an operational claim; zero records means unknown, not normal; no synthetic severity score, legal conclusion, or causal attribution is generated by Core.

Migration: `0014` (additive).
