# Platform Core v2.43.0 — Evidence Integrity & Chain of Custody

This Open Forensics release extends v2.42 evidence provenance with governed chain-of-custody records. It adds custodians, deterministic hash-chained custody events, seal/unseal state, content-integrity verification, continuity assessments, immutable custody snapshots, and external attestation references.

Core records and verifies the internal integrity of custody records. It does not independently verify a person's identity, a physical handoff, evidence authenticity, ownership, legal admissibility, criminal attribution, or any legal conclusion.

Migration: `0047`.
