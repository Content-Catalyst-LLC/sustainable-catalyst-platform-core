# Platform Core v3.75.0

## Knowledge Graph Representation Learning

Adds entity/relation representation spaces, scoring-function contracts, negative-sampling provenance, training lineage, triple-score records, and filtered ranking evaluation.

Invariant: **representation score ≠ graph fact ≠ evidence**. Missing links used as negative samples are not false facts. v3.75 does not validate candidate relationships or promote scored triples to evidence edges.
