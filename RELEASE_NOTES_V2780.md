# Platform Core v2.78.0 — Hypothesis & Competing Explanation Engine

Adds first-class hypothesis sets, researcher-authored hypotheses, assumptions, testable predictions, explicit evidence assessments, declared hypothesis-to-hypothesis relations, discrimination gaps, hypothesis revision history, descriptive comparison matrices, and immutable hypothesis snapshots.

This release builds directly on v2.77.0 Finding, Claim & Evidence Intelligence. Hypotheses can retain references to structured findings and claims while preserving a strict boundary between evidence, researcher interpretation, and explanatory hypotheses.

Core does **not** generate hypotheses, infer evidence positions, compute a hypothesis score, rank explanations, select a preferred explanation, confirm or reject hypotheses, infer causality, determine truth, optimize the research path, or publish conclusions. Comparison matrices report only explicit researcher-authored records and descriptive counts.

Database lineage: **0082**, additive after **0081**.
