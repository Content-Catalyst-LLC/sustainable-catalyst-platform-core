# Platform Core v2.42.0 — Forensic Object Model & Evidence Provenance

Begins Open Forensics as a cross-platform capability layer. Adds governed investigations, forensic objects, evidence items, source bindings, provenance activities, semantic relations, immutable snapshots, provenance graphs, and portable investigation packages.

This release records evidence origin and integrity metadata but does not establish legal chain of custody, authenticity, attribution, causal responsibility, guilt, or legal conclusions.
