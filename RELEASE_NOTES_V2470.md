# Platform Core v2.47.0 — Media Artifact & Derivative Provenance

Adds migration `0051` and seven additive tables for governed media provenance. Original/derivative labels and transformation lineage are explicit recorded assertions, not automated authenticity findings. Media comparison records are descriptive and may preserve externally produced metrics while Core remains non-executing and non-determinative.
