# Platform Core v2.45.0

## Forensic Timeline & Event Reconstruction

- Adds migration `0049` and seven additive forensic timeline tables.
- Adds evidence-linked events with exact, bounded, approximate, or unknown temporal precision.
- Adds event participants and explicit temporal/event relations.
- Adds reconstruction hypotheses with assumptions and unresolved conflicts.
- Adds saved timeline views, renderer-neutral specifications, and immutable snapshots.
- Preserves explicit non-inference, non-ranking, non-verdict, and non-truth-promotion boundaries.
