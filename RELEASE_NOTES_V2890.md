# Platform Core v2.89.0 — Research Quality, Bias & Methodological Audit Engine

Adds a governed methodological-audit layer over protocols, executions, evidence, findings, claims, inferences, publications, replications, and syntheses. Systematic audit criteria include confounding, selection/measurement bias, missing data, unsupported causal language, statistical method concerns, uncertainty, citation/evidence traceability, contradictory findings, protocol deviations, and reproducibility.

Core records assessor- or external-system-declared audit results and provenance. It does not infer bias, score research quality, rank studies, determine method/causal validity, resolve contradictions, verify citations automatically, certify reproducibility/ethics, reject research, or determine truth.
