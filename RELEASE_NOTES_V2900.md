# Platform Core v2.90.0 — Research Workflow & Orchestration Engine

Adds governed research lifecycle workflows with ordered stages, declared transitions, cross-product context bindings and handoffs, checkpoints, events, policies, revisions, lineage, and immutable snapshots. Core may apply an explicitly declared transition and maintain canonical workflow state, but it does not choose research paths, advance autonomously, execute specialist work, dispatch external jobs, infer completion, generate findings/claims, approve scientific validity, resolve challenges, rank paths, or determine truth.
