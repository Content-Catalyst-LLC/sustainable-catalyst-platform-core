# Platform Core v2.78.0 Audit

## Capability

Hypothesis & Competing Explanation Engine provides governed hypothesis sets, researcher-authored hypotheses, assumptions, predictions, evidence assessments, declared relations, discrimination gaps, descriptive comparison matrices, revisions, and immutable snapshots.

## Research boundary

The engine preserves human research judgment. Core records explicit positions and computes only deterministic descriptive counts from those records. It does not generate hypotheses, infer support or contradiction, score or rank hypotheses, select a winner, confirm or reject explanations, infer causality, or promote any hypothesis to truth.

## Provenance

Hypotheses may reference v2.77 findings and claims by stable reference, while all v2.78 objects preserve project identity, provenance metadata, creation metadata, and revision/snapshot lineage.
