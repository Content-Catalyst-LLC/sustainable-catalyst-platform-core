# Platform Core v2.84.0 — Research Program & Longitudinal Knowledge Graph

Adds governed research programs spanning multiple research projects, program objectives and milestones, declaration-based longitudinal knowledge graph nodes and edges, time-indexed knowledge-state records, research-evolution events, revision history, provenance lineage, descriptive program/timeline summaries, and immutable hash-chained snapshots.

Core preserves researcher-authored and source-backed longitudinal records. It does not prioritize research, allocate funding, rank projects, automatically link graph entities, infer research direction or causality, forecast program success, resolve evidence gaps, or infer truth.
