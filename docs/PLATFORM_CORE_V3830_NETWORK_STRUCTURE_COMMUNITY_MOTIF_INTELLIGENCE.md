# Platform Core v3.83.0 — Network Structure, Community & Motif Intelligence

## Purpose

v3.83.0 defines governed contracts for descriptive network analysis over immutable, provenance-bound graph snapshots. It extends v3.82 relationship discovery without converting analytical graph structure into factual, evidentiary, causal, affiliational, or misconduct conclusions.

## Evidence and interpretation boundaries

Analytical edges preserve their upstream epistemic state. Source-observed relationships, candidates, and hypotheses remain distinguishable inside network snapshots. Centrality is not importance or evidence strength. Community membership is not affiliation. Motif participation is not coordination or causality. Bridge/broker scores are not influence or intermediary facts. Structural equivalence is not identity or shared intent. Network structure is not evidence of wrongdoing.

## Runtime boundary

Platform Core defines contracts and validates lineage. Large-scale graph computation belongs in specialist runtimes such as Workspace or Research Lab. Runtime outputs may not mutate the evidence or identity graph and may not promote a relationship.
