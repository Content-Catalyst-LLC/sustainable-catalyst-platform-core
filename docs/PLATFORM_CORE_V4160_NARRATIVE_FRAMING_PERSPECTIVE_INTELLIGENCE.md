# Platform Core v4.16.0 — Narrative, Framing & Perspective Intelligence

Platform Core v4.16.0 adds governed source-perspective, framing-signal, narrative-frame, cross-perspective comparison, framing-assessment, provenance, and reproducible snapshot objects above v4.15 causal-context intelligence.

## Boundaries

- Framing analysis is descriptive, not truth adjudication.
- Framing difference is not automatically factual contradiction.
- Emphasis is not manipulation; omission is not deception.
- Perspective does not infer private motive or ideology.
- Framing score is not credibility, bias, or truth.
- Original-language authority and translation derivation remain explicit.
- Causal and evidence review authority remain upstream.
- No graph mutation and no database migration.
