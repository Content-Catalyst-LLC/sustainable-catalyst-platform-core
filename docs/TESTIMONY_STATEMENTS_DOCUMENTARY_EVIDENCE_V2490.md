# Platform Core v2.49.0 — Testimony, Statements & Documentary Evidence

Adds governed testimony/statements, documentary records and assertions, explicit source context and locators, speaker/author references, claim bindings, corroboration/contradiction relations, temporal-consistency records, and immutable documentary snapshots.

Core preserves assertions and analyst-declared relationships. It does not verify speaker identity, determine authorship, score credibility, automatically extract claims, detect corroboration, or promote statements to truth.
