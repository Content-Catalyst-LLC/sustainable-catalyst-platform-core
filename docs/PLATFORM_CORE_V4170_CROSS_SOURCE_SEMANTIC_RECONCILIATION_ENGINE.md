# Platform Core v4.17.0 — Cross-Source Semantic Reconciliation Engine

Platform Core v4.17.0 adds a governed reconciliation layer over the v4.16 narrative/framing context. It creates source-preserving semantic anchors, correspondence candidates, explicit conflicts, review decisions, navigation clusters, provenance records, and reproducible snapshots across claims, actors, terminology, temporal scope, spatial references, multilingual variants, framing context, evidence context, and causal context.

## Governance boundary

Reconciliation is alignment, not canonicalization. Similarity, shared keys, source agreement, temporal overlap, spatial grounding, or a review decision do not establish truth, identity, equivalence, source credibility, evidence validity, or a graph fact. Original-language representations remain authoritative; translations remain derived and cannot count as independent sources. Unresolved identity, contradictions, scope differences, modality differences, cultural-semantic divergence, and source-independence constraints remain explicit.

## Mutation boundary

No canonical entity merge and no Context Graph, Evidence Graph, Knowledge Graph, or Identity Graph mutation is authorized or performed by this release.
