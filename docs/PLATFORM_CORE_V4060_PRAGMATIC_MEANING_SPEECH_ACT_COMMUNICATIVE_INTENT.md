# Platform Core v4.6.0 — Pragmatic Meaning, Speech Act & Communicative Intent

v4.6.0 adds governed pragmatic interpretation on top of v4.5.0 Epistemic, Modal, Negation & Certainty Semantics.

## Objects

- Pragmatic source excerpts and immutable content spans
- Communicative participants with contextual speaker/audience roles
- Pragmatic cues and genre/register context
- Speech acts: assertion, request, recommendation, warning, commitment, and extensible additional act types
- Communicative intents with explicit confidence, review state, and provenance
- Pragmatic interpretations and immutable/supersedable snapshots

## Boundaries

Speech-act classification does not establish proposition truth. Requests do not create platform obligations; recommendations do not establish normative correctness; warnings do not establish risk as fact; commitments do not guarantee future performance. Communicative intent is an interpretation of observable language and context, not privileged access to a speaker's private mental state. Genre/register labels are not authority or credibility scores. Surface speaker/audience forms do not establish canonical identity.

The embedded v4.5 predecessor remains immutable and no identity, evidence, context, or knowledge graph mutation is performed.

Database migration: none.
